Manage your Raspberry Pi from a modern web dashboard

PiSTG is a lightweight interface server for your Raspberry Pi. Monitor system stats, manage files, and control users — all from your browser.

Everything you need

A full toolkit for your Raspberry Pi

PiSTG brings the tools you'd normally reach for over SSH into one clean, fast, web-based interface.

Live System Monitoring

Track CPU, memory, disk, and temperature in real time with animated charts that update as your Pi runs.

File Management

Browse, upload, download, and organize files on your Pi through a clean, familiar file explorer.

User Management

View and manage users on your device. See profiles, roles, and activity at a glance.

Remote Settings

Tweak system settings and preferences from anywhere — no monitor or keyboard attached to your Pi.

Secure by Default

Authenticated access keeps your device protected. Your Pi, your rules, safe from prying eyes.

Instant Notifications

Get alerted when system events happen, so you always know what's going on with your device.

The dashboard

See your Pi's health at a glance

A real-time overview of everything running on your device. Clean gauges, live charts, and instant status — all in one screen.

  • Animated, auto-refreshing charts
  • One-click access to files & users
  • Works in any modern browser
